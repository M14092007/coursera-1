Welcome to our little shop

Bean & Brew started as a two person roasting project in a tiny garage. Six years later we still roast five kilograms at a time, so every bag is checked by hand before it reaches the shelf.

We keep the menu short on purpose. Four single origin beans, one house blend, and a pastry case that changes with the season. If you are not sure what to order, just ask the person behind the bar.

What we are known for

Slow bar

Pour over, siphon and cold drip are all made to order at the end of the counter. It takes a few extra minutes, but you get to watch the whole thing.

Fresh pastry

Croissants come out of the oven at 7:00 and 14:00. Banana bread and cookies are baked every morning until the tray runs out.

Quiet corner

The back room has six tables, plenty of plugs and no music after 18:00. It is the best place in the neighbourhood to finish a report.
